9Drive Storage Gateway

9Drive is a self-hosted storage management application that connects Google Drive accounts into one dashboard.

Features

  • Connect multiple Google Drive accounts
  • Upload and download files
  • Manage folders and storage quotas
  • Organize and share stored files

Google Drive Permissions

9Drive requests Google Drive access to display, upload, download, organize, and manage files in connected accounts. Access is granted through Google OAuth.
