9Drive Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This privacy policy describes how this independently hosted 9Drive Storage Gateway installation handles user information and connected Google Drive accounts.

1. Information We Process

The application processes account information, including names, email addresses, and login credentials. When you connect Google Drive, it may process file metadata, file contents, storage quota information, and OAuth tokens.

2. Google Drive Access

Google Drive access is requested through Google OAuth with your authorization. The application uses this access to list, upload, download, organize, share, and manage files in your connected Google Drive account. Depending on your actions, this can include modifying or deleting files.

3. Storage and Processing

This installation uses Cloudflare Pages for its frontend, Hostless for its application backend, and TiDB Cloud for its database. File content is stored in the connected Google Drive or other storage destination. Application data, metadata, and connection credentials may be processed or stored by these services.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

Information is processed by the hosting and storage services necessary to operate 9Drive. Files may also be made accessible to recipients when you use the application's sharing features.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Application data may remain stored until it is deleted through the application or by the installation administrator, subject to applicable backups and provider retention policies. Disconnecting a Google account may not delete files already stored in it.

6. Revoking Google Access

You can revoke Google Drive access through your Google Account's third-party connections: Google Account Connections . You can also use the Disconnect option in 9Drive where available.

7. Security

The installation uses HTTPS for browser connections and relies on its hosting, application, and database security controls. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Google API Data

The application's use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's features, service providers, or data handling practices change.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the operator of this 9Drive installation using the contact information provided by the operator.
